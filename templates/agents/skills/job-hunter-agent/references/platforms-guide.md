# Supported Job Platforms & ATS Guide

This guide outlines how the **Job Hunter & Auto-Apply Agent** interacts with supported job sites and ATS forms.

---

## 1. Local & Regional Job Portals
- Search keywords relevant to the candidate's target roles and framework experience.
- Handles direct apply forms, company redirect links, and email CV submissions.

---

## 2. Global & International Platforms

### A. LinkedIn Jobs & Easy Apply
- **Target URL:** `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/`
- **Search Filters:**
  - Keywords: `QA Automation Engineer`, `SDET`, `Lead QA Automation`, `Playwright`, `Software Engineer`
  - Location: `Worldwide / Remote` or local country
  - Filter: `Easy Apply`
- **Autofill:** Auto-populate 1-page/multi-step dialogs (Phone, Email, Experience years, CV selector).

### B. ATS Direct Platforms (Lever, Greenhouse, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Ashby)
- **Lever (`jobs.lever.co`):** Single-page form (Name, Email, Phone, Resume upload, Custom questions).
- **Greenhouse (`boards.greenhouse.io`):** Multi-field form (EEO, Work auth, Custom tech questions).
- **Workable & SmartRecruiters:** Standard field mappings.

### C. Multi-Source Aggregator Platforms
- Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Google Jobs — scraped and aggregated via the unified aggregator engine.
