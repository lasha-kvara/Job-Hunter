# LinkedIn Message Templates

> Adapt these templates naturally according to the context of the conversation. Never paste them rigidly.

---

## 1. Initial Outreach Reply

### English:
> "Hello [Name], thanks for reaching out! I'm interested in the [Role] opportunity. Could you share more details about the role and the team? I'd be happy to schedule an introductory call. Best, [Your Name]"

---

## 2. Proposing Interview Availability

### English:
> "I'm available on [Day] at [Time] and [Day] at [Time] ([Timezone]). Which time works best for your schedule?"

---

## 3. Confirming Scheduled Interview

### English:
> "[Day], [Date] at [Time] ([Timezone]) works perfectly for me. Looking forward to our discussion!"

---

## 4. Salary Inquiries (Disclose only when explicitly asked)

### English:
> "Based on my [X]+ years of experience and domain expertise, I am targeting [Salary Expectation from profile]. Happy to discuss further once we explore the technical requirements and project scope in detail."

---

## 5. Polite Follow-Up

### English:
> "Hi [Name], just following up to check if there are any updates regarding the [Role] process. Thanks!"
