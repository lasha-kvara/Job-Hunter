# LinkedIn Session Limits

LinkedIn restricts accounts that behave like bulk automation. Keep usage modest and personal.

## Limits per session
- Open at most **5** conversations.
- Keep the active session under **20–25 minutes**.
- One action per HR turn: read OR send, never several messages in a row.
- Do not reopen the same conversation repeatedly in one session.

## Behaviour
- Wait for pages to finish loading before acting; do not click disabled buttons.
- Use the normal UI (search bar, Messaging tab) like a regular user.

## Stop conditions
- On a CAPTCHA, "unusual activity" or verification checkpoint: stop immediately, tell the user,
  and let them handle it. Do not try to get around it.
